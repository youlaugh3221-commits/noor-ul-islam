Namaz / Salah

A beginner-friendly outline. Learn the exact recitations and details with a trusted teacher, since some details differ between schools of jurisprudence.

1. Prepare

Make wudu, ensure the place and clothes are clean, cover the required area, face the Qibla, and make the intention in your heart.

2. Begin standing

Stand calmly and say the opening takbir: “Allahu Akbar”. Recite Al-Fatihah and the additional recitation as taught.

3. Ruku

Bow with calmness, then rise to standing.

4. Sujud

Prostrate, sit briefly, and make the second prostration with calmness.

5. Complete the prayer

Continue the required rak‘ahs, sit for tashahhud, and finish with salam according to the method you are learning.

This is an overview, not a complete prayer manual. Ask a qualified local imam or teacher to demonstrate and check your practice.

Tahajjud prayer

Tahajjud is a voluntary night prayer performed after ‘Isha and after sleeping, before Fajr. Learn the timing and method from a trusted scholar in your tradition.

1. Timing

The night extends from after ‘Isha until Fajr. The last third of the night is a commonly recommended time.

2. Intention and prayer

Make the intention in your heart and pray voluntary units calmly. There is flexibility in the number of units.

3. Witr and dua

Learn how Witr fits into your night prayer routine according to your school, and make personal dua.
